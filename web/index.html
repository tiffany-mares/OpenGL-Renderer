cube

The OpenGL-Renderer demo, compiled to WebAssembly — single-threaded and browser-paced, on purpose.

Click the cube to give it keyboard focus, then: Space pauses the spin  ·  ← → yaw  ·  ↑ ↓ pitch.

What you are not seeing

This build deliberately removes the two systems the project is actually about. The frame pacer is meaningless in a browser: there is no high-resolution sleep, busy-spinning the main thread would freeze the page, requestAnimationFrame owns the frame clock, and performance.now() is coarsened to ~100 µs without cross-origin isolation — the native build's headline <200 µs pacing result cannot even be measured here. The threaded render does not port either: Emscripten's offscreen-canvas paths (OFFSCREEN_FRAMEBUFFER / OFFSCREENCANVAS_SUPPORT) only support HTML5-API or SDL2 contexts, not GLFW — so there is no render thread, by decision rather than omission. What remains is the shared GL scene on one thread, paced by the browser.

The pacer benchmarks, the input-handoff benchmarks, and the threaded architecture live in the native build: source and full writeup on GitHub.
